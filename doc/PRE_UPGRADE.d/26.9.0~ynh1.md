## Upgrading to Continuwuity v26.9.0

Upstream disabled **legacy unauthenticated media** by default in this
release (`allow_legacy_media` now defaults to `false`). Authenticated
media downloads, uploads and thumbnails keep working normally, so most
servers won't notice any change. If you (or a third-party bridge/script)
relied on unauthenticated `/download` URLs, those will stop working after
the upgrade. This is intentional — it prevents your homeserver from being
used as an open CDN.
